## Classification principle

We should avoid treating all Indian philosophical categories as if they were the same kind of thing. A future metadata layer should distinguish at least:

- **tradition** — broad historical or religious tradition
- **school** — organized philosophical system
- **movement** — religious or social movement
- **lineage** — transmission or community lineage
- **position** — analytical classification used for comparison

This distinction will become important when connecting Indian traditions to `schools/`, `people/`, `concepts/`, `questions/`, and `debates/`.
